# Polish declension (`pol`)

Author(s): Magdalena Markowska

Source(s): UniMorph

## Summary

This data consists of Polish nom.sg. forms and the corresponding gen.sg. Many of the forms exhibit "yer" (fleeting _e_ or _ie_) or _o_/_u_ alternations in the stem, and there are several competing gen.sg. suffixes. Rubach, Buckley, and Dąbrowska, respectively, have argued that these three patterns are generally unpredictable.

## Third column

The third column contains inherent gender/animacy information, encoded according to the UniMorph specification, which is relevant to the selection of the relevant gen.sg suffixes.

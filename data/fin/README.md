# Finnish consonant gradation (`fin`)

Author(s): Kyle Gorman

Source(s): Silfverberg et al. 2021 (via Turku Dependency Treebank and OmorFi)

## Summary

This data consists Finnish nom.sg. forms and the corresponding gen.sg. The nom.sg. forms are roughly the 5,000 most frequent nom.sg. forms in the Turku Dependency Treebank (Haverinen et al.) and the gen.sg. form was generated using the OmorFi morphological analyzer/generator (Pirinen). Finnish nouns may exhibit interparadigmatic stem-final alternations known as _gradation_; for instance the _pappi_ 'priest' has a gen.sg. _papin_, in which the geminate _pp_ has degeminated ("quantitative gradation), and _sopu_ 'agreement' has a gen.sg. _sovun_, with _p_ converted to _v_ ("qualitative gradation"). This phenomenon has been discussed extensively by linguists and is inspired by the recent paper by Silfverberg et al.

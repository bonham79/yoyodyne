Author(s): Selin Alkan, Kyle Gorman

Source(s): TELL, UniMorph

## Summary

This data consists of Turkish nom.sg. forms and the corresponding "definite accusative" (acc.sg) and 3rd person singular possessive. The vowel in this suffix usually harmonizes with the stem, and the addition of a vowel-initial suffix may trigger velar deletion or, in words of (Perso-)Arabic origin, loss of a fleeting vowel in the final syllable of the stem.

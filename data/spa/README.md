# Spanish conjugation (`spa`)

Author(s): Mariana Graterol, M. Elizabeth Garza

Source(s): LEXESP, UniMorph

## Summary

This data consists of Spanish verbs (in their infinitive citation form) and their 1sg. present indicative. Many of the forms feature diphthongization of _e_ or _o_ to _ie_ or _ue_, respectively, the "raising" of _e_ to _i_, or the insertion of a stem-final _c_ or _g_. Harris has argued that these patterns are generally unpredictable. This example is inspired by Albright et al.
